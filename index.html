FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Do I need to be home?

No. Most of our clients give us a key, a code, or a lockbox. Whatever you're comfortable with is fine, and we'll confirm the arrangement in writing.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes — everything, including vacuums and microfiber. If you'd rather we use your products, we're happy to.

How is pricing decided?

By the size and condition of the home, not by the hour. You get one flat price before we start, so the number never changes on you.

Are you insured?

Casa Bonita LLC is a registered business carrying general liability insurance. We can send you a certificate on request.

What about pets?

We love them. Tell us their names and anything we should know, and we can switch to pet-safe, low-fragrance products.

How do I pay?

Cash, check, Zelle, Venmo, or card. Payment is due after the visit — never before.
