Quotes and pricing

Quotes are free and are based on the size and condition of the home. We give one flat price before any work starts, and that price doesn't change afterwards.

If the home turns out to be substantially different from what was described — much larger, or in much heavier condition — we'll stop and talk to you before continuing rather than quietly charging more. You can decline and owe nothing.

Scheduling and access

A slot is held once you accept a quote, not when you send a request. You don't have to be home; most clients arrange a key, a code, or a lockbox. However you'd like us to get in, we'll confirm it with you in writing beforehand.

If we can't get in at the scheduled time and can't reach you, we may have to treat it as a late cancellation.

Cancellations

Let us know at least [24 hours] ahead and there's no charge. Later than that, or if we arrive and can't get in, we may charge [a $__ fee / 50% of the visit]. We're reasonable about genuine emergencies.

If we have to cancel on you, we'll tell you as early as we can and reschedule at your convenience.

Payment

Payment is due after the visit, never before. We accept [cash, check, Zelle, Venmo, card].

Our re-clean promise

If something wasn't done well, tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and redo it at no charge. This is a re-clean, not a refund — we'd rather fix the work than argue about it.

What we don't do

For everyone's safety, our staff don't: move heavy furniture or appliances, climb higher than a two-step stool, handle biohazards, clean up after pests or rodents, or use ladders outdoors.

Please put away cash, jewelry, medication, and anything irreplaceable before we arrive. Let us know in advance about anything fragile or valuable that needs special care, or that you'd rather we didn't touch at all.

Damage

If we break something, tell us right away and we'll make it right. Please report any damage within [48 hours] of the visit so we can see what happened while the facts are fresh.

Normal wear, pre-existing damage, and items that were already loose, cracked, or failing are not something we can take responsibility for.

Photos

We sometimes photograph our work. Nothing from your home goes on this website or anywhere else without your explicit permission, and we never show faces, documents, mail, or anything that identifies where you live. You can say no, or change your mind later and we'll take it down.
