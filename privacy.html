Who we are

Casa Bonita LLC, a limited liability company operating in [CITY, STATE]. You can reach us at hello@casabonita.example or (555) 123-4567.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us. If you fill in the quote form, that is:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address, if you give one
  • The service you're asking about and the size of your home
  • Anything you write in the message box

If you text, call, or email us instead, we have whatever that message contains, including your phone number or email address.

We do not run analytics software on this site. We do not have a tracking pixel, an advertising tag, or a visitor counter. Nobody is building a profile of you here.

What we do with it

We use it to answer you, give you a price, and schedule a visit. That's all. We do not sell it, rent it, trade it, or hand it to anyone for marketing. We do not add you to a mailing list.

We would only share information if we genuinely had to — for example, if the law required it, or if we needed it to resolve a dispute about work we did for you.

How long we keep it

Quote requests we didn't take on are kept for about [12 months] and then deleted. Records for actual customers are kept as long as needed for scheduling, billing, and tax records.

Cookies and storage

This site sets no cookies and does no tracking. It does store one small setting in your own browser — whether you're reading in English or Spanish — so it can remember your choice on your next visit. That stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Things loaded from elsewhere

The headings on this site use a typeface served by Google Fonts. Your browser requests the font file from Google, which means Google sees your IP address as part of that request. We receive nothing from it. If you would rather that request didn't happen at all, it can be removed — the font would simply be served from our own site instead.

The quote form

Right now the form doesn't transmit anything by itself. When you submit it, your own phone or email program opens with the details filled in, and you send the message. That means your request reaches us the same way any text or email does, and no third-party service sits in between.

If this changes — for example if the form starts posting to a form-handling service — this section has to be updated to name that service. Do not skip that.

Your choices

You can ask us what we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@casabonita.example and we'll do it. You don't have to give a reason.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy law — for example, California residents have rights under the CCPA. We'll honor those requests regardless of where you live.

Children

This site is meant for adults arranging cleaning services. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, the date at the top changes with it.
